Where the numbers come from

Each calculator states its assumptions under the result, so you can check the math or swap in your own figures.

U.S. Department of Energy
Federal efficiency standards (10 CFR 430) behind the refrigerator, freezer, furnace and light bulb numbers.
NOAA
Heating degree days and winter design temperatures for 25 U.S. cities.
U.S. Energy Information Administration
Average and regional prices for electricity, natural gas, propane and heating oil.
U.S. Environmental Protection Agency
Carbon dioxide per kilowatt-hour, therm and gallon of fuel.
NREL
Peak sun hours and production assumptions for solar sizing.
USDA
Food safety timelines for freezers and refrigerators during outages.
