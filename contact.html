Reporting an Error

Accuracy matters most to us. If a result or figure looks wrong, it helps to include:

  • The page address (URL)
  • The values you entered and the result you got
  • What you expected, and a source if you have one

We check every report against the original data source. When a correction is needed, we fix the page and update its date.
