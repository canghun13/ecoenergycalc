Your Propane Use

Heating uses the same model as our heating cost calculator: 8, 18, 28 or 40 thousand BTU per square foot a year by climate, divided by furnace efficiency, at 91,500 BTU per gallon. The monthly split follows NOAA 1991–2020 heating degree day normals for Chicago (Minneapolis for very cold). Water heating is about 62 gallons per person a year, a range or oven about 50 gallons, a dryer about 25 and a fireplace 150–300, based on propane supplier estimates; fireplace use is assumed to fall between October and April, peaking in December and January. Tanks are filled to 80% and refilled at 30%.

How Often You'll Need a Delivery

Propane tanks are filled to about 80% of their size to leave room for expansion, and most homeowners reorder around 30%, so each delivery is about half the tank. A 500-gallon tank takes about 250 gallons a fill. A 2,000 sq ft cold-climate home with an 80% furnace, water heater and range uses about 1,065 gallons a year, which works out to about four deliveries; in January alone it burns about 183 gallons, so one delivery lasts about six weeks. A 250-gallon tank would need refilling roughly every three weeks in January, which is why most homes that heat with propane use a 500-gallon tank or an automatic delivery plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much propane does a house use per month in winter?

For a 2,000 sq ft home with a 95% furnace, heating takes about 86 gallons in January in a mixed climate like Chicago and about 133 gallons in a cold climate, less in November and March. An 80% furnace uses about a fifth more. Add roughly 20–25 gallons a month for a propane water heater for a family of four.

How many gallons of propane does a 2000 sq ft house use per year?

For heating alone, about 410–490 gallons in a mixed climate, 640–770 in a cold climate and 920–1,090 in a very cold one, depending on furnace efficiency. With a water heater and range for a family of four, add about 300 gallons a year.

How long does a 500 gallon propane tank last in winter?

Each delivery is about 250 gallons, because tanks are filled to 80% and usually reordered around 30%. A cold-climate 2,000 sq ft home that uses about 180 gallons in January with heat, hot water and cooking gets about six weeks from a delivery in January and longer in spring and fall.

How much propane does a water heater use per month?

About 20 to 25 gallons a month for a family of four, or roughly 200 to 300 gallons a year according to propane supplier estimates. Usage rises in winter because the incoming water is colder, and drops with low-flow fixtures or fewer people.

How many times a year do you fill a propane tank?

A home that heats with propane and uses about 1,000 gallons a year needs about four deliveries with a 500-gallon tank, or eight with a 250-gallon tank. Homes using propane only for a water heater, range or fireplace may need one or two fills a year.
